# 15 — Vertical Apps — Knowledge Graph, Node Editor, Desktop & Table

> Engine primitives are correct in isolation; verticals prove they compose under paging, undo, windowing, and 100k-row pressure. A table that only works at 10 rows, a graph that teleports on expand, or a window that leaks its overlay a11y mirror are all app-level composition bugs, not physics or renderer bugs — and the forge keeps them separate for a reason.

- **What you'll learn**: how four verticals compose stable primitives — `KnowledgeGraphModel` over `GraphLayout`/`Graph3D`, `NodeEditor` over `CommandHistory`/`SelectionState`/`layoutDocument`, `DesktopShell`/`WindowManager`/`DesktopWindow` over `Scene` overlays, and `Table` over `Text` + `GridCellHotspot` virtualization — with every file boundary and ownership rule that makes incremental growth cheap and teardown clean.
- **What you won't**: the physics inside `ForceLayout2D`/`VectoForceLayout` (boss 11), the VMT dirty lifecycle (boss 06), or renderer/DPR contracts (boss 07). This doc shows how apps _consume_ those engines, not how the engines compute.

## 1. Knowledge Graph — a paginated cut over 3D

### 1.1 Data contract

`KgEntity extends GraphNode` at `packages/knowledge-graph/src/types.ts:19` and `KgFact extends GraphLink` at `types.ts:31` so the same objects flow straight into `@vectojs/graph3d` layouts and renderers with domain fields untouched (`type`, `labels: LabelMap`, `predicate`, `confidence`, `provenance`). `KgDataSource` at `types.ts:54` is the lazy seam: `getNodes(ids?)` for seeds and `getNeighbors(id, { limit, cursor, direction, signal })` at `types.ts:58` for paged hops. `KgNeighborhood` at `types.ts:68` carries `facts`, `neighbors`, `nextCursor`/`hasMore`, and an optional `entity` — absence means "unknown id" and must fail, not fabricate (see §1.3).

`LabelMap` at `types.ts:12` is `Record<languageTag, string>` with `''` as fallback; `pickLabel` at `types.ts:87` prefers the requested language, then `''`, then `en/zh/…`, then an arbitrary key. `KgGraphData` at `types.ts:43` is the in-memory snapshot after adapter materialization.

`MemoryDataSource` at `packages/knowledge-graph/src/MemoryDataSource.ts:15` is the test/small-graph adapter: indexes facts by both endpoints (`out`/`inn` at `MemoryDataSource.ts:17`) so `getNeighbors` is `O(degree)`, dedups self-loops in `'both'` (`MemoryDataSource.ts:71`), and versions cursors as `"<version>:<offset>"` (`MemoryDataSource.ts:108`) — `load()` at `MemoryDataSource.ts:26` bumps `version` so in-flight cursors fail loudly instead of slicing a mutated list (`MemoryDataSource.ts:125`).

`rdf.ts:11` provides `parseRdfTurtle(text)` via `n3` `Parser`: subjects become entities, `rdf:type` sets `type` (last wins, `rdf.ts:51`), `rdfs:label`/`skos:prefLabel`/`schema:name` fill `labels` (`rdf.ts:56`), other object-IRI triples become `KgFact`s (`rdf.ts:62`), and every entity gets an `''` fallback (`rdf.ts:74`). Sync `Parser.parse` — not for multi-hundred-MB on the main thread (`rdf.ts:24` doc).

### 1.2 FixedZLayout — 2D projection without a fork

`FixedZLayout` at `packages/knowledge-graph/src/FixedZLayout.ts:22` wraps `VectoForceLayout` and clamps every `z` to a constant after each `step()` (`FixedZLayout.ts:49`) and after `setGraph` (`FixedZLayout.ts:37`). The inner simulation still runs as a 3D Barnes-Hut octree; `pinNode` delegates with `z ?? this.z` (`FixedZLayout.ts:56`), `sanitize()` at `FixedZLayout.ts:85` reseeds non-finite `x/y` onto a `cbrt` spiral. Pin contract diverges from `ForceLayout2D` (ID-addressed): `FixedZLayout` pins by index like `GraphLayout` (`GraphLayout.ts:46`), noted at `FixedZLayout.ts:18`.

### 1.3 KnowledgeGraphModel — the single layout driver

`KnowledgeGraphModel` at `packages/knowledge-graph/src/KnowledgeGraphModel.ts:62` is renderer-neutral and owns the paginated cut: `entities`/`facts`/`factKeys`/`expansions`/`requests`/`entityOrder`/`lastPositions` (`KnowledgeGraphModel.ts:69`). It is the **single driver** of its borrowed `GraphLayout` (`KnowledgeGraphModel.ts:43` doc: one `setGraph` per `rebuildGraph`, one `reheat` per `expand`). Construction takes `source`, optional borrowed `layout`, `pageSize`, `direction`, `lang` (`KnowledgeGraphModel.ts:39`).

- `bootstrap(focusIds, expandSeeds)` at `KnowledgeGraphModel.ts:114` fetches seeds via `getNodes`, `ingestEntities`, `rebuildGraph()`, then `expand` each seed.
- `expand(id)` at `KnowledgeGraphModel.ts:127` shares a promise per id (`KnowledgeGraphModel.ts:134`), short-circuits on `complete` (`KnowledgeGraphModel.ts:136`), marks `loading` (`KnowledgeGraphModel.ts:144`), then `loadPage` (`KnowledgeGraphModel.ts:240`).
- `loadPage` pages via `source.getNeighbors` with `AbortSignal` (`KnowledgeGraphModel.ts:246`), fails loudly if `page.entity` is missing (`KnowledgeGraphModel.ts:259` — never ingest a placeholder `'Unknown'` node), ingests entities/facts, advances `loaded` by **batch** `page.facts.length` not net-new (`KnowledgeGraphModel.ts:273`), records `ExpansionState` (`KnowledgeGraphModel.ts:275`), `rebuildGraph()` + `layout?.reheat(0.5)` (`KnowledgeGraphModel.ts:285`).
- `cancelExpand` at `KnowledgeGraphModel.ts:150` aborts via `AbortController`, marks `cancelled`.
- `rebuildGraph()` at `KnowledgeGraphModel.ts:332` captures layout positions, merges in stable `entityOrder`, seeds new nodes from `lastPositions`, builds `GraphData` with `pickLabel`, and calls `layout?.setGraph`.
- `dispose()` at `KnowledgeGraphModel.ts:225` deliberately does **not** dispose the borrowed layout — the session may still share it (`KnowledgeGraphModel.ts:230` comment).

Snapshot contract at `KnowledgeGraphModel.ts:23` is `{ version:1, entities, facts, expansions }`; `exportSnapshot`/`importSnapshot` preserve positions via `lastPositions` and revision-bump to abort stale requests (`KnowledgeGraphModel.ts:190`).

### 1.4 Graph3D + GraphLayout — the rendering seam

`GraphLayout` at `packages/graph3d/src/layout/GraphLayout.ts:12` is a minimal worker-friendly contract: `setGraph(data)`, `step(iterations) -> boolean` active/hot (`GraphLayout.ts:28` doc), flat `Float32Array positions` of xyz triplets in `GraphData` node order (`GraphLayout.ts:35`), optional `pinNode`/`unpinNode`/`reheat` by index. `Graph3D` at `packages/graph3d/src/Graph3D.ts:28` is two draw calls regardless of size: one `InstancedMesh` (`Graph3D.ts:115`) with `∛val` radius scaling (`Graph3D.ts:104`), one `LineSegments` (`Graph3D.ts:136`). `setGraphData` validates endpoints before attaching (`Graph3D.ts:73`) and `applyPositions` at `Graph3D.ts:149` writes matrices, tracks bounds inline (avoiding `computeBoundingSphere` cost — `Graph3D.ts:178` measured 60–78%), and warns once on short arrays (`Graph3D.ts:162`).

### 1.5 KnowledgeGraphSession — wiring

`KnowledgeGraphSession` at `packages/knowledge-graph/src/KnowledgeGraphSession.ts:67` owns `model`, `graph: Graph3D`, `camera: GraphCamera`, `layout: GraphLayout`, and `interaction: GraphInteraction`. The model is constructed with borrowed layout + `lang` (`KnowledgeGraphSession.ts:120`). Interaction wires `camera: () => camera.camera` so mode swaps stay live (`KnowledgeGraphSession.ts:129`), with `handleSelect`/`handleHover` routing to index-aligned `entityByIndex` (`KnowledgeGraphSession.ts:87`).

- `bootstrap` at `KnowledgeGraphSession.ts:182` awaits `model.bootstrap`, bails if disposed mid-flight (`KnowledgeGraphSession.ts:189`), then `syncFromModel()` + `camera.fitToPositions`.
- `syncFromModel()` at `KnowledgeGraphSession.ts:287` mirrors `model.getGraphData()` into `graph.setGraphData`/`applyPositions` and `interaction.setNodeCount`.
- `tick(iterations)` at `KnowledgeGraphSession.ts:242` steps layout, captures layout positions only when settled (`KnowledgeGraphSession.ts:250` — hot frames are not cached), applies to renderer. `tick` returns settled (`!stillHot`) matching `if (!tick()) rAF`.

Accessibility: `knowledge-graph` does **not** project per-node DOM (`KnowledgeGraphSession.ts:64` doc) — pair with an aggregate `role="status"` announcer in the host.

### 1.6 Frame integration and lifecycle

The session never owns the `WebGLRenderer` or the `requestAnimationFrame` loop — the host does (`KnowledgeGraphSession.ts:60` doc). Correct wiring is `attach(scene)` once (`KnowledgeGraphSession.ts:153`), `bootstrap` awaited (`KnowledgeGraphSession.ts:182`), then per-frame `tick()` + `render(renderer, scene)` (`KnowledgeGraphSession.ts:242`/`KnowledgeGraphSession.ts:256`). `tick` captures only settled positions (`KnowledgeGraphSession.ts:250` — hot-frame captures would write one `Map` entry per node per frame) and returns `true` when cooled (`KnowledgeGraphSession.ts:252`), so the loop is `if (!session.tick()) requestAnimationFrame(loop)` (`KnowledgeGraphSession.ts:240` doc). Post-await disposal guards at `KnowledgeGraphSession.ts:189`/`KnowledgeGraphSession.ts:227` quiesce late continuations from the constructor's fire-and-forget `bootstrap` (`KnowledgeGraphSession.ts:145`) — without them the mirror runs against a torn-down `Graph3D`.

`loadSnapshot` at `KnowledgeGraphSession.ts:202` is the demo/offline path: it builds a `KnowledgeGraphSnapshot` with every entity marked `complete` and `loaded = facts.length` (`KnowledgeGraphSession.ts:208`) so `expandOnSelect` (`KnowledgeGraphSession.ts:94` option, `KnowledgeGraphSession.ts:318` check) does not re-fetch hops already present. `setSize` at `KnowledgeGraphSession.ts:263` forwards to `camera.setSize`; `getMode` at `KnowledgeGraphSession.ts:160` exposes the session's `mode` (`'2d'|'3d'` at `types.ts:6`).

### 1.7 Graph data types and host ownership

`GraphData` at `packages/graph3d/src/types.ts:1` is `{ nodes: GraphNode[], links: GraphLink[] }` where `GraphNode` carries `id: NodeId` (`types.ts:6` `string|number`), optional `x/y/z` seeds, optional `fx/fy/fz` pins, `val` for `Graph3D` radius, and `color`. `GraphData` is the only object that crosses the model→layout→renderer seam — `KnowledgeGraphModel` builds it from `KgEntity`/`KgFact` (`KnowledgeGraphModel.ts:332` `pickLabel` + `position` spread), `GraphLayout.setGraph` consumes it by value (implementations clone or copy into SoA), and `Graph3D.setGraphData` indexes it by id (`Graph3D.ts:80` `indexById`). The renderer is deliberately ignorant of how positions were computed (`Graph3D.ts:26` doc: swappable or remote behind a worker), and the layout is agnostic of how the renderer batches (`VectoForceLayout.ts:68` doc). Host ownership is explicit: the caller constructs `VectoForceLayout`/`D3ForceLayout`/`FixedZLayout` and `Graph3D`/`GraphCamera`, the model borrows the layout (`KnowledgeGraphModel.ts:47` `layout?: GraphLayout` doc), and the session owns it (`KnowledgeGraphSession.ts:277` disposes). `D3ForceLayout` clones into `simNodes` (`D3ForceLayout.ts:71`) because d3 mutates its inputs; `VectoForceLayout` keeps f32 SoA (`VectoForceLayout.ts:88` `positions/vx/vy/vz/fx/fy/fz`) and never mutates caller nodes. `Graph3D.applyPositions` short-circuits on `positions.length < count*3` with a once-per-`setGraphData` warning (`Graph3D.ts:162` `hasWarnedShortPositions`, reset at `Graph3D.ts:100`) rather than writing NaN instance matrices that make the whole mesh frustum-culled.

## 2. Node Editor — document, commands, selection

### 2.1 Document model

`NodeDocument` at `packages/node-editor/src/model.ts:54` is `{ nodes: NodeData[], links: LinkData[] }` with immutable transforms (`cloneDocument` at `model.ts:78`, `deepCloneValue` at `model.ts:64` for `data` maps, `updateNodePosition` at `model.ts:93`). `NodeData` carries `position`, optional `width`/`height`, `ports: PortDefinition[]` (`model.ts:8` with `direction`, `dataType`, `maxConnections`), `data`; `LinkData` carries `source`/`target` + port ids (`model.ts:27`).

`validateLink` at `model.ts:126` checks source/target presence, `same-node` self-loop reject, `duplicate-link-id`, port presence + direction (`output`→`input`), `incompatible-types` on `dataType`, `duplicate-link` on directed endpoint pair, and `target-port-occupied` via `maxConnections` (`model.ts:152`). Cycles spanning multiple nodes are **allowed** — the doc is a user-authored flow (`model.ts:117` doc). `addLink`/`removeLink`/`removeNode` at `model.ts:163` enforce it; `removeNode` at `model.ts:178` removes incident links to stay referentially valid.

### 2.2 History and selection

`CommandHistory` at `packages/node-editor/src/history.ts:9` is a textbook undo/redo: `execute(label, after)` snapshots `before`/`after` via `cloneDocument`, pushes onto `undoStack`, clears `redoStack` (`history.ts:28`), with `undo()`/`redo()` swapping `current` (`history.ts:40`). All `NodeEditor` mutations go through this single gate so every user action is one history entry.

`SelectionState` at `packages/node-editor/src/selection.ts:9` tracks `ids: Set<string>` with `select(id, additive)` (`selection.ts:22`), `clear()`, `list()` snapshot, and a transient `drag: DragState | null` (`selection.ts:11`) for the current drag. `layoutDocument` at `packages/node-editor/src/layout.ts:14` is a deterministic layered layout: Tarjan SCC (`layout.ts:96` `stronglyConnectedComponents`) over the component DAG, Kahn topological rank assignment (`layout.ts:54`), then `x = originX + rank*horizontalGap`, `y = originY + index*verticalGap` (`layout.ts:85`). Deterministic by sorting ids and targets (`layout.ts:18`/`layout.ts:27`).

### 2.4 Known traps in this vertical

Editor-specific pitfalls that have cost real review time (see `packages/node-editor/src/editor.ts` comments inline):

- **Pointer provenance filter** — `PortEntity` click handler at `editor.ts:90` gates on `nativeEvent instanceof KeyboardEvent` because core also synthesizes `click` for native browser clicks on the same mirror (pointer capture retargets a released connect-drag back onto it). Without the filter, a released connect would both complete and re-arm a pending keyboard connection.
- **Drag aliasing** — `moveDrag` mutates `documentState`'s own node object (`editor.ts:427` "owns its node objects via `cloneDocument`") so the in-place edit cannot alias a history snapshot; `dragDocument` (the pre-drag clone at `editor.ts:411`) stays clean for `cancelDrag`.
- **History contamination** — `endDrag` only pushes when the JSON changed (`editor.ts:445` `JSON.stringify(before) !== JSON.stringify(current)`), and `applyAutoLayout` the same (`editor.ts:276`). A no-op drag otherwise adds an undo entry that does nothing but shift the redo stack.
- **Transient gesture scope** — `deleteNodes` at `editor.ts:390` and the `Ctrl+Z/Y` path at `editor.ts:474` both cancel pending `connection`/`drag` before mutating history, so the history command sees the true document, not a half-moved node.

## 6. App traps vs engine defects — the forge split and the dogfooding loop

`vectojs-docs/forge/findings/app-level-and-toolchain.md:1` names the split explicitly: app-level findings are _not_ engine defects but are kept because diagnosis cost real effort. The `forge/findings/README.md` template plus the per-area index makes `app-level-and-toolchain` vs `core-*`/`simulation-*`/`text-*` unmistakable at review — ambiguous entries drift into engine triage and waste repair budget. Status is append-only (`app-level-and-toolchain.md:5`), with `Upstream status` updated when a fix ships (`app-level-and-toolchain.md:8`).

Representative app traps, each a vertical composition lesson:

- **Sandboxed iframe opaque origin** (`app-level-and-toolchain.md:30` 2026-07-16): a motif gallery `sandbox="allow-scripts"` without `allow-same-origin` gives an opaque origin; ES-module + `importmap` fetches become CORS and a same-origin `./demo.js` never loads — blank with no parent console error. Fix: `allow-scripts allow-same-origin` + real static-route `src` (`app-level-and-toolchain.md:51`), not `srcdoc` (resolves to `about:srcdoc`, `app-level-and-toolchain.md:48`). Lesson for verticals: trusted first-party embeds need a real origin; untrusted code needs a separate subdomain, not a silent iframe attribute change.
- **Canvas count ≠ booted** (`app-level-and-toolchain.md:66`): host polled `querySelectorAll('canvas').length >= 2` assuming a second WebGL point-layer canvas means "ready" — canvas2D demos and `ThreeAdapter` (offscreen `canvas`, not in DOM) never reach 2, so the "Loading…" overlay never hid. Fix: `canvas.width > 0` backing-store check (`app-level-and-toolchain.md:80`). Lesson: vertical readiness probes must key off backing-store size, not DOM canvas count; backend count is an implementation detail.
- **EPUB image pipeline** (`app-level-and-toolchain.md:94` 2026-07-19 trio): `body.textContent` silently drops `<img>`/SVG `<image>` (`app-level-and-toolchain.md:99`), so manga EPUBs render empty; interpolated `alt` without `escapeMarkdown` lets `foo](javascript:…)` become a clickable link via `marked` reparsing (`app-level-and-toolchain.md:155`); and `data:image/jpeg;base64,…` tokenized by `[a-zA-Z0-9]+` splits at every `+`/`/` into ~20k tokens, typing out base64 as gibberish for minutes (`app-level-and-toolchain.md:185`). - **Lex coalescing + `hasPendingAnimations`** (`app-level-and-toolchain.md:222`): `MathMarkdown.appendMarkdown` fired `marked.lexer` per chunk (`O(n²)` over accumulated text, `app-level-and-toolchain.md:236`), and `StreamReader` lacked `hasPendingAnimations()`, so an `onDemand` scene idled mid-stream when `update()` added zero characters (`app-level-and-toolchain.md:246`). ## 7. Traps worth checking before you land a vertical change

1. **Borrowed-layout dispose** — `KnowledgeGraphModel.dispose()` must not dispose the borrowed layout (`KnowledgeGraphModel.ts:230`); `KnowledgeGraphSession.dispose()` must (`KnowledgeGraphSession.ts:277`). Swapping one for the other leaks or double-frees. `attach(scene)` at `KnowledgeGraphSession.ts:153` is idempotent and must be called before `render(renderer, scene)` at `KnowledgeGraphSession.ts:256` or it throws `call attach first`.
2. **Cursor staleness** — `MemoryDataSource` cursors version (`MemoryDataSource.ts:118`); `KnowledgeGraphModel.expand` dedup (`KnowledgeGraphModel.ts:134`) and post-await disposal bail (`KnowledgeGraphSession.ts:189`/`KnowledgeGraphSession.ts:227`) are the fences against mutating mid-pagination. `loadSnapshot` at `KnowledgeGraphSession.ts:202` marks every entity `complete` so select does not re-fetch already-present hops.
3. **Port direction + identity** — editor `validateLink` rejects same-node and wrong-direction ports (`model.ts:131`/`model.ts:138`); connection preview `isConnectionTarget` mirrors it via try-`addLink` (`editor.ts:360`/`editor.ts:549`). `findPortAt` at `editor.ts:502` walks reverse add-order so overlapping cards resolve to the topmost port, and `getLocalPoint` via `worldToLocal` at `editor.ts:498` keeps drag/hit in editor-local space, not `sceneX/Y`.
4. **Overlay a11y leak** — `WindowManager.focus`/`close` must `releaseA11yProjection` on blur (`WindowManager.ts:248`) and `DesktopWindow` must stay `pointerEvents none` (`Window.ts:400`) or the dialog mirror eats chrome clicks. Chrome buttons need `a11yProjection eager` (`Window.ts:383`) or the first click misses (next-frame `onDemand` projection), and `DisplayLayout.clampRect` (`DisplayLayout.ts:89`) must be used for every placement (`WindowManager.ts:149`).
5. **Table viewport math** — `reconcileVirtualRows`/`_syncGridA11y` share the same `first/last` bound (`Table.ts:397`/`Table.ts:662`) with `overscan`; `clampScrollPosition` at `Table.ts:344` keeps the spring offset from overshooting the clamped target; `rowTops` prefix at `Table.ts:751` keeps classic a11y `O(rows)` not `O(rows²)`. `pageRows()` at `Table.ts:548` uses mean `rowHeights` in classic mode and `viewportHeight - headerHeight` in virtualized, so `PageUp/PageDown` stay viewport-sized in both.
6. **Publishing runtime deps** — framework deps belong in `peerDependencies` + `workspace:*` in `devDependencies` (`app-level-and-toolchain.md:467`); `npm publish` ships `dependencies` verbatim (`app-level-and-toolchain.md:473`). Verify via a tarball-consumer probe (`bun add` in a scratch dir) before a forge app consumes a new minor.
7. **Selection + history coupling** — `deleteNodes` at `editor.ts:390` ends transient gestures first and batches nodes+incident links as one undoable command; `cancelConnection`/`cancelDrag` before `history.execute` prevents dangling `dragDocument` or pending connections. `applyAutoLayout` at `editor.ts:274` pushes only when the JSON changed, so a no-op layout does not pollute the undo stack.
8. **Persistence round-trip** — `exportDocument` at `persistence.ts:168` validates structurally _and_ semantically (`validateDocument` at `persistence.ts:84` with per-link `validateLink` at `persistence.ts:156`); `importDocument` at `persistence.ts:178` rejects wrong `schemaVersion` (`persistence.ts:186`). Bypassing `exportDocument` to hand-write JSON that passes only the structural half can still fail `validateLink` for `target-port-occupied` or `incompatible-types` on re-import — always round-trip through the same validator.

## 7a. Cross-vertical patterns worth stealing

Four verticals, four answers to the same question — "how does incremental growth stay cheap and reversible?":

- **Knowledge Graph: borrow, don't clone.** `KnowledgeGraphModel` never copies a layout — it borrows one (`KnowledgeGraphModel.ts:43` `layout?: GraphLayout` doc, "Borrowed, not owned"), drives it with `setGraph` + `reheat`, and leaves disposal to the session (`KnowledgeGraphSession.ts:277`). Any new vertical that wraps a shared engine (physics, text shaping, media decode) should copy this split: the model's `dispose` at `KnowledgeGraphModel.ts:225` is six lines of map/set clears with an explicit comment "leaves it untouched so it can stay shared with a live session" (`KnowledgeGraphModel.ts:230`). Forgetting the comment is how a future editor re-adds `this.layout?.dispose()` and breaks the session.

- **Node Editor: history owns the document.** Every mutation — `createLink` (`editor.ts:380` → `addLink` + `history.execute`), `deleteNodes` (`editor.ts:390` → batched `removeNode` + one `history.execute`), `moveDrag` (`editor.ts:441` → `history.execute('Move node', …)`), `applyAutoLayout` (`editor.ts:274` → `history.execute('Auto-layout', …)`) — goes through `CommandHistory.execute` (`history.ts:28`) which snapshots `before`/`after` via `cloneDocument` and clears `redoStack`. The editor never mutates `documentState` without going through `applyDocument` (`editor.ts:520` → `cloneDocument` + `rebuild` + `markDirty`). Steal this for any future canvas editor: one gate, one clone discipline, no out-of-band `documentState = …`.

- **Desktop: overlays own focus, not the scene.** `WindowManager` never calls `Entity.remove` to restack (`WindowManager.ts:339` `kids.splice` + `markStructureChanged`) because `remove` detaches a11y and unregisters drivers — thrashing on every focus change. It tracks focus via `focused` + `releaseA11yProjection`/`requestA11yProjection` (`WindowManager.ts:248`/`WindowManager.ts:253`) so `onDemand` background windows do not keep a permanent a11y mirror. The desktop `Wallpaper` is `a11yProjection never` (`DesktopShell.ts:26`), `DesktopWindow` is `onDemand` with `pointerEvents none` on its dialog mirror (`Window.ts:400`), and `Taskbar` is `eager` because its buttons must be reachable without a hover (`Taskbar.ts:58`). Any new shell surface should pick one of these three modes deliberately, not default to `eager`.

- **Table: window, pool, integrate.** Virtualization is three cooperating budgets: `reconcileVirtualRows` windows exactly `overscan`-padded `[first, last]` (`Table.ts:397`/`Table.ts:404`), `_syncGridA11y` pools `RowHotspot`/`GridCellHotspot` to `need` (`Table.ts:691`), and `update(dt)` integrates `scrollY` with a dt-aware spring (`Table.ts:352` `velY += diff*7.2*(dt/1000)` / `exp(-dt/84)`). `hasPendingAnimations` at `Table.ts:378` is the idle-throttle handshake that makes all three survive `renderMode onDemand`. A future virtualized list that copies only the window step but forgets `hasPendingAnimations` will scroll correctly once and then stall — the same bug `StreamReader` had at `app-level-and-toolchain.md:222`.

## 7b. Verification — how to prove a vertical change is correct

Verticals are app-shaped, so the proof is app-shaped too — not just unit tests.

- **Knowledge graph:** `just test-pkg knowledge-graph` covers `MemoryDataSource` cursor versioning (`MemoryDataSource.ts:118` stale-cursor throw) and `KnowledgeGraphModel` dedup/reheat (`KnowledgeGraphModel.ts:134`/`:285`); add a repro that calls `model.expand(id)` twice concurrently and asserts one promise, then `cancelExpand` and assert `cancelled` state. For rendering, drive `KnowledgeGraphSession` in a headed browser (not `jsdom`) and assert `graph.group.children.length` after `syncFromModel` — `Graph3D` is Three.js and needs a real canvas.

- **Node editor:** `just test-pkg node-editor` exercises `validateLink` error codes (`model.ts:36` `LinkValidationError`) and `exportDocument`/`importDocument` round-trip (`persistence.ts:168`); add a case that at-capacity `maxConnections` passes when the link is its own occupant (`persistence.ts:156` strip-self logic) and a drag case that asserts `history.canUndo` stays false after a no-op drag (`editor.ts:445`).

- **Desktop:** `just test-pkg desktop` covers `AppRegistry` and `DisplayLayout.clampRect` (`DisplayLayout.ts:89`); add a dialog-modality case that calls `wm.openDialog({modal:true})` then `wm.focus(other)` and asserts focus stays on the dialog (`WindowManager.ts:237` topModal gate), and a `restack` case that asserts `overlayRoot.children` order without `Entity.remove` (`WindowManager.ts:339`).

- **Table:** `just test-pkg table` covers `normalizeColumnWidths` (`Table.ts:789`) and `seenCells` duplicate rejection (`Table.ts:228`); add a virtualized case that sets `viewportHeight`, calls `appendRows` 10k times, scrolls to `maxScroll()` (`Table.ts:328`), and asserts `mountedRows.size` is `O(viewport)` not `O(rows)` (`Table.ts:392` window), plus a roving-tab case that asserts `isGridTabStop` re-anchors after the focused row scrolls out (`Table.ts:668`).

In every case run `just check` (oxfmt + oxlint + markdownlint) before pushing — vertical docs live beside code and share the same gate (`AGENTS.md:31` quality gates).

## 8. Checklist — before you land a vertical change

1. **Own your disposal.** `KnowledgeGraphModel` borrows, `KnowledgeGraphSession` owns (`KnowledgeGraphModel.ts:230` vs `KnowledgeGraphSession.ts:277`); `DesktopShell.dispose` at `DesktopShell.ts:251` tears down `shortcuts`/`windowManager`/`taskbar`/`wallpaper` in order. Grep every `dispose` you touch for borrowed vs owned.
2. **Guard every async continuation.** `bootstrap`/`expand` both bail on `this.disposed` post-await (`KnowledgeGraphSession.ts:189`/`KnowledgeGraphSession.ts:227`); `expand` dedups by id (`KnowledgeGraphModel.ts:134`) and `cancelExpand` aborts (`KnowledgeGraphModel.ts:150`). A new `await` without a guard reintroduces the late-mirror bug.
3. **Project a11y structurally.** `Table` is `role grid` with `RowHotspot` rows (`Table.ts:55`) and `GridCellHotspot` cells (`Table.ts:82`) pooled and `pointerEvents none`; `DesktopWindow` is `role dialog` with `pointerEvents none` (`Window.ts:400`) and eager chrome buttons (`Window.ts:383`); `NodeEditor` ports are `role button` with keyboard provenance (`editor.ts:90`). A new interactive child without `pointerEvents none` on its structural parent steals hits.
4. **Keep `hasPendingAnimations` honest.** `Table` at `Table.ts:378` and `StreamReader` after the fix at `app-level-and-toolchain.md:259` both return `true` while animating so `onDemand` never idles mid-gesture. Any new `update(dt)` that calls `markDirty` internally needs the same override.
5. **Validate before you publish.** `packages/desktop` broke on `workspace:*` in `dependencies` (`app-level-and-toolchain.md:455`); `packages/tex` broke on `sideEffects: false` (`app-level-and-toolchain.md:407`). Both survived CI because `vitest` aliases `src/` and no consumer probe ran. Add a tarball `bun add` smoke test to the release checklist.
6. **Record the finding in the right forge bucket.** `app-level-and-toolchain.md:1` is append-only (`app-level-and-toolchain.md:5`) — never edit an existing entry, never file an app trap under `core-*`/`simulation-*` (`forge/findings/README.md` classification). The split is what keeps engine triage from drowning in app noise, and the dogfooding loop (`vectojs-native/*/AGENTS.md`) depends on it.

## 9. Future verticals and extension points

New verticals should start from the seam that already exists, not from a fork.

- **Knowledge graph extensions.** Swap `KgDataSource` for a worker-backed or streaming source (`types.ts:54` `MaybeAsync<T>` at `types.ts:84` already allows sync or async): page size, `direction: 'out'|'in'|'both'` (`types.ts:64`), and `signal` abort are the only knobs the model needs. A timeline or map vertical reuses `KnowledgeGraphModel` with a different `GraphLayout` (e.g. a 1D temporal `FixedZLayout` variant or a `ForceLayout2D` projection) and a different `Graph3D` style — `GraphLayout` + `GraphData` are the contract, not the force law.

- **Node editor extensions.** `PortDefinition.dataType` (`model.ts:12`) + `maxConnections` (`model.ts:13`) already model typed sockets; `layoutDocument` options (`layout.ts:3` `originX/Y`, `horizontalGap`/`verticalGap`) are the only layout knobs. A shader-graph or audio-graph vertical adds `NodeData.type` branches and `validateLink` `incompatible-types` rules (`model.ts:140`) without touching `history.ts` or `selection.ts`. `CommandHistory` is intentionally label-bearing (`history.ts:4` `label`) so an undo log is human-readable.

- **Desktop extensions.** `AppRegistry.register` at `AppRegistry.ts:15` is the plugin seam (runtime registration without touching `WindowManager`), and `openDialog` at `WindowManager.ts:184` is the transient seam. A new app needs only `AppDefinition.create: (ctx: AppContext) => Entity` (`types.ts:1`) — `ctx` carries `scene`, `vfs`, `windowManager`, `close`, `appId`, `windowId`. `ShortcutRouter` (`ShortcutRouter.ts:1`) already namespaces `custom` actions so an app can add chords without colliding with `open-app`/`close-focused`.

- **Table extensions.** `Table` is already generic over `TableCell = string | Entity` (`Table.ts:4`), so a chart-cell or sparkline vertical passes an `Entity` cell and owns its own `width/height` — `fitCell`/`setCellSelectable` probe by capability (`Table.ts:5`), not by type tag. Virtualization stays `O(viewport)` as long as new cells stay sized through `setMaxWidth` (`Table.ts:6`). `appendRows` (`Table.ts:885`) is the only mutation — a `setRows` would need a11y re-anchoring and `seenCells` invalidation, which is why it is deliberately absent (`Table.ts:870` doc).

In every case the cost of a new vertical is one new package plus a `GraphData`/`NodeDocument`/`AppDefinition`/`TableCell` adapter — not a new engine.
